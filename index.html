The Craft of Going Higher

An educational guide to mountaineering — techniques, history, hazards, and ethics of climbing high places.

Explore Topics Safety First
Why This Guide

Mountains reward patience more than ambition.

Good mountaineering is less about conquering summits and more about judgement: reading the weather, knowing your team, and turning around when conditions say so.

This guide gathers the core ideas a new climber meets — from basic rope work to the history that shaped modern practice — in plain, careful language.

Safety Maxim

"The summit is optional. Getting down is mandatory."

A widely repeated mountaineering maxim by Ed Viesturs. Before any climb, study safety procedures, understand mountain weather, and learn from certified professionals.
