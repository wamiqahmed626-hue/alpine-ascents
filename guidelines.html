Guidelines for Responsible Climbing

Essential expedition preparation, gear checklists, and Leave No Trace principles

Pre-Climb Planning

The Five Golden Rules of Preparation

1
Progressive Training

Build stamina and rope reflexes incrementally on local crags and training peaks before tackling extreme objectives.

2
Route & Weather Research

Study topographic maps, read previous trip reports, and check multi-day meteorological and avalanche reports.

3
Permits & Evacuation Insurance

Secure legal park climbing permits and high-altitude helicopter rescue insurance with medical repatriation.

4
Leave a Route Plan

Provide basecamp contacts or family with your intended route, planned bivouac spots, and hard overdue search deadlines.

5
Firm Turnaround Time

Fix an ironclad turnaround time (e.g., 1:00 PM). Turn back immediately when reached, regardless of how close the summit appears.

Mountain Ethics

Leave No Trace in High Places

Pack It Out

Never leave food wrappers, broken equipment, or spent oxygen canisters behind. Use designated human waste bags in high snow zones.

Respect Communities

Honor indigenous customs and mountain spiritual sites. Ensure fair wages, comprehensive insurance, and safe gear for local support crews.

Protect Terrain

Stick to existing trails in valley approaches and avoid damaging fragile alpine tundra that takes decades to recover from trampling.

Aid Distressed Teams

The ancient unwritten law of mountaineering: assist fellow climbers in emergency distress whenever it is safe for your party to do so.
